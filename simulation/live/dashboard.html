    What each node measures

    • Flat node20, one per flat

      Water flow meter and pressure sensor. Raises: water running at night, water never stopped for 8 hours, much more water than usual.

    • Tank node1, on the roof tank

      Inflow and outflow meters, water level, pH, TDS (dissolved salts), turbidity (cloudiness) and temperature. Raises: water leaving the tank much faster than usual, runoff or chemical in the water (small model on the node), supply running low.

    • Gateway1, outside

      No sensors. Receives every message, notices nodes that go quiet, and every night from 2 to 4 am compares the water that left the tank with what all the flats used: anything missing is leaking from the pipes.
