                What each node measures

                • Flat node20, one per flat

                  Water flow meter and pressure sensor. Flats 301, 201, 101 and G01 also read the down pipe meter above their floor. Raises: water running at night, water never stopped for 3 hours, much more water than usual, high flow with the flat's pressure dropping. Says when each problem is over.

                • Tank node1, on the roof tank

                  Inflow and outflow meters, water level, pH, TDS (dissolved salts), turbidity (cloudiness) and temperature. Raises: water breaking the drinking water limits (pH, TDS, turbidity), what the bad water looks like (runoff or chemical, small model on the node), water above 35 °C, water leaving the tank much faster than usual, supply running low. Shuts the tank valve on bad water; a person reopens it after testing.

                • Gateway1, outside

                  No sensors. Receives every message, notices nodes that go quiet, and for each floor's stretch of down pipe compares the water in, the water on to the next floor and what the 4 flats used: more than 30 L missing in 15 minutes, or 20 L between 2 and 4 am, is a leak on that floor. Sends a beacon every minute, and warns flats when the tank valve shuts or their floor's pipe leaks.

                Reading the drawing

                • Room colour: normal
                • Room colour: an alert from this node reached the gateway
                • Room colour: node not heard for 45 min
                • Leak running (the system still has to find it)
                • Red light on a node: its own alarm, buzzer beeping, works even with the radio down
                • Yellow light: the node's messages are waiting for the gateway's reply
                • Blue light: a warning from the gateway (tank valve shut, or a leak on this floor's pipe)
                • Tank valve on the pipe leaving the tank: green open, red shut
                • Spot on the down pipe where you can start a leak; red once the leak is found
                • Rising pipe, pump to tank
                • Down pipe, tank to flats
                • Flow meter on the down pipe, one above each floor
                • Heights are drawn 2.6 times taller than real so the floors don't hide each other
